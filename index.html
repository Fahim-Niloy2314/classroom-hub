B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering Degree Requirements

The undergraduate curriculum consists of 8 semesters across 4 academic levels (Level 1 to Level 4). A student must earn a minimum of 160.00 credits with a minimum CGPA of 2.20, complete industrial training / internship, and successfully defend the final undergraduate thesis project to graduate.
